## Personalization
Create your own card from A to Z while discovering fun facts about how real cards are made at every step.
# Interaction
Visitors move through 10 playful steps, each revealing a short fun fact about how cards are made from paper and corners to symbols and shine.
At every step, they perform a simple gesture (like cutting, stamping, or choosing) to unlock the next stage and gradually build their own personalized card.
In the end, they can see or print their unique creation a card full of fun and discovery.
